Rihla (رحلة) — built-in Kingdom map data (web/data/ksa.js)
============================================================

ksa.js is generated by build/ksa_data.py from two sources:

1) Natural Earth (https://www.naturalearthdata.com, https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector)
   Land, country borders, roads, railways and airports.
   Public domain — "Made with Natural Earth. Free vector and raster map data @ naturalearthdata.com."

2) Saudi Arabia Regions, Cities and Districts — by homaily
   https://github.com/homaily/Saudi-Arabia-Regions-Cities-and-Districts
   Regions, cities/villages and district boundaries with Arabic and English names
   (collected by the author from the Saudi National Address portal).
   Licensed under the GNU General Public License v2.0 — full text in GPL-2.0.txt next to this file.
   The derived data in ksa.js (simplified and re-encoded geometry, same names) is distributed under
   the same GPL-2.0 licence; the program that produced it (build/ksa_data.py) is in the public
   repository https://github.com/shabiot2/Rihla .

Street map tiles shown while online come from OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL) via CARTO basemaps;
viewed tiles are kept on the device for at most 29 days, following the provider's terms.
